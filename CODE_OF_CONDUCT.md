# Code of conduct

Orphic follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:
- be respectful and welcoming;
- disagree about ideas, not people;
- no harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that break these rules.

Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting (Security tab), or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.
